About

I'm a 3rd-year M.Tech (Integrated) Computer Science & Engineering student at SSN College of Engineering. I work across the full stack — from systems-level networking tools and IoT hardware to fine-tuned language models and deep learning pipelines.

My research has been recognised at MediaEval 2025 (3rd place, GI Imaging VQA) and DravidianLangTech 2026 at the ACL workshop (Top 10, Tamil NLP). I'm currently preparing for internships and further research publications.

I'm drawn to hard problems: routing anomalies in live networks, pixel-level change detection in aerial imagery, abusive language in low-resource Dravidian languages. I care about getting the details right.

BLIP-2 VQA with Multimodal Explanations for Gastrointestinal Imaging

MediaEval 2025 — Subtask 2  ·  CEUR Workshop Proceedings

Applied the BLIP-2 vision-language model to the Kvasir-VQA-x1 dataset for visual question answering on endoscopy images. Generated answers with natural-language explanations, attention maps, and per-answer confidence scores. Achieved 3rd place internationally.

IndicBERT for Abusive Tamil Comment Classification

DravidianLangTech 2026 — ACL Workshop  ·  Draft Prepared

Fine-tuned IndicBERT for multi-class Tamil abusive language detection on social media. Applied class weighting and threshold optimisation to handle severe label imbalance. Achieved Macro F1 of 0.8001, placing in the top 10 of the shared task.

Hull Tactical Market Prediction — Kaggle

Kaggle Competition  ·  ML & Quantitative Finance

ElasticNet regression model with feature engineering, EWM imputation, StandardScaler, and 10-fold cross-validation. Trading signals optimised using Sharpe ratio, achieving a final Sharpe score of approximately 3.99.
